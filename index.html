What's in the case

Home-style recipes in the tradition of Villanueva, Zacatecas. Coming for something specific? Call ahead and ask.

Pan dulce

  • ConchasSweet buns under a crackled sugar shell
  • EmpanadasGuava and cream cheese
  • Rosca de ReyesThe Three Kings ring bread
  • Pan de muertoFor Día de los Muertos, in season

Calavera and pumpkin conchas, for Halloween and Día de los Muertos. One fall, a handwritten board in the shop listed pumpkin cheesecake conchas and pan de muerto filled with Nutella or cajeta.
